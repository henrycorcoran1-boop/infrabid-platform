01 · Services

Every discipline your project needs, under one roof

From the first walk of the site to the final certificate, CKCE carries design, survey, assessment and statutory sign‑off in‑house — so nothing is lost between consultants.

  • Civil & structural design

    Foundations, superstructures, retaining works and drainage design for new builds and extensions — calculated, detailed and coordinated with your architect from day one.

  • Self‑build supervision

    Stage inspections and interim certificates for mortgage drawdown, so your lender releases funds on schedule and your build stays on a sound footing.

  • GPS & topographical surveys

    GNSS/RTK topographical surveys, setting out, boundary resolution and land registry compliant mapping — survey‑grade data you can design and register against.

  • Percolation & site suitability

    Trial holes and percolation testing to the EPA Code of Practice 2021 for domestic waste water treatment systems — the assessment your planning application depends on.

  • Planning applications

    Pre‑planning consultations with the local authority, drawings, reports and lodgement — applications built to answer the planner's questions before they are asked.

  • Feasibility & site appraisals

    Buying a site or weighing an extension? We appraise access, services, ground conditions and flood risk screening before you commit, and tell you plainly what the site can carry.

  • Structural assessments

    Structural surveys of existing buildings and Opinions on Compliance with planning permission and building regulations — for sales, purchases and lending requirements.

  • BCAR & project management

    Design Certifier and Assigned Certifier roles under BCAR (S.I. No. 9 of 2014), plus tender and procurement support and project management from feasibility to handover.

02 · How we work

A measured line from first call to final certificate

One point of contact throughout. You always know what stage you are at, what happens next, and what it costs.

STAGE 01

Consult

We meet on site or by phone, understand what you want to build, and set out scope, timeline and fee in plain terms.

STAGE 02

Survey & assess

GNSS survey, trial holes, percolation testing and site appraisal establish exactly what the ground and the planners will allow.

STAGE 03

Design

Civil and structural design, drainage and planning drawings — coordinated, costed and lodged with the local authority.

STAGE 04

Certify & deliver

Stage inspections, BCAR certification and interim certificates for your lender, through to Opinion on Compliance at handover.

04 · Why CKCE

A decade of drawings, certificates and ground truth

Established in 2016, CKCE has grown on repeat clients and referrals — the kind of reputation you only earn by getting planning granted, structures right and certificates issued on time.

  • Local knowledge that shortens the road

    We work with Laois County Council and the Midlands planning authorities week in, week out. We know what each planner expects to see — and we lodge applications that show it.
