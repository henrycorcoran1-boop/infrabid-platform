06 — The ask

Decisions required from leadership

Four decisions needed to hold the September and October dates

Each of these sits outside the AWDS team's control and is currently absorbing float.

  1. 01
    M142 recovery plan and resources
    Approval of the recovery plan and the additional resources it requires, ahead of the 19 Aug IDC.
  2. 02
    ESB and Uisce Éireann interfaces
    Confirmation of ESB diversion dates and the Uisce Éireann interface positions. Two of the top five risks depend on this.
  3. 03
    Land access information
    M138 is proceeding to assurance without full land access constraint information and M146 utility delays threaten IDC readiness. Confirm the route to that information, or accept the design assumption risk.
  4. 04
    M130 evaluation and Design Certificate
    Confirm the review team and procurement evaluation dates, and the route for Design Certificate issue, so the Tender Evaluation Report can be closed out.
